## A seat for the fans

Welcome to The Fish Box, an independent home for Baton Rouge Kingfish hockey. This is a place for game recaps, opinions, and conversations about the team and the people in the stands.

The idea is simple: keep the schedule handy, keep up with the official team updates, and give fans something to talk about between games.

## What’s coming

As the season gets going, look for articles, reactions, and the occasional rant. There is plenty of room here for a good hockey conversation.

Have a question or an idea for a story? The contact section will open soon. Until then, welcome in. Let’s talk hockey.
