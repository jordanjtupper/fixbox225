We have a little more information about the FPHL’s visa situation. And apparently, getting everybody cleared to play comes with a pretty serious bill.

According to the report, the league has applied for P-1A visas for professional athletes and coaches. Watertown Wolves owner Dan Drake says the goal is to get players properly authorized to be here and paid to play in the FPHL.

The reported cost is more than $2,000 per player, with nearly another $3,000 to request faster processing. Drake says the league is covering those expenses.

That gives us a clearer picture of what’s happening behind the scenes. There is an application process underway and money being committed to it. The part everybody wants, an actual arrival date, is still missing.

Drake said the league had hoped to hear back the previous week and was hoping for an answer that week. The report describes expedited processing as taking 15 business days, but that shouldn’t be read as a guarantee that every player will be dressed and ready two weeks later.

For the Kingfish, this update doesn’t identify who is still waiting or confirm when anyone joins the lineup. It does give us more reason to keep watching the roster.

Meanwhile, Baton Rouge opened with two wins over Monroe. The guys already on the ice have given us plenty to enjoy while the paperwork gets sorted out.

If more of the planned roster becomes available, Jimmy Soper could have some interesting decisions to make. After a weekend like that, earning those minutes should require more than showing up with an approved application.

We’ll take the wins now. Hopefully, some clearer answers follow.
