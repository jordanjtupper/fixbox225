The Baton Rouge Kingfish gave the Fresno Falcons everything they could handle Saturday night, but it wasn't quite enough.

Fresno came away with a 4–3 overtime victory at Selland Arena, completing a two-game weekend sweep and handing Baton Rouge its second consecutive loss.

After Friday's 4–2 defeat, the Kingfish needed a better performance against one of the FPHL's strongest early-season teams. They got one, battling back to force overtime and earning a standings point in the process.

Unfortunately, Fresno's Aaron Huffnagle ended things 2:49 into the extra period.

## A Better Fight, But the Same Result

Tobias Odjick, Tyler Larwood, and Jake Cox provided the goals for Baton Rouge, giving the Kingfish enough offense to push Fresno beyond regulation.

The Falcons, though, continued to demonstrate why they've been such a difficult opponent early in the season.

Baton Rouge also had to navigate another goaltending change. Anthony Seykora started the game but was replaced by Edward Coffey, who finished with 38 saves.

Coffey's performance helped keep the Kingfish competitive and gave them an opportunity to steal a second point before Huffnagle's overtime winner settled things.

It was a frustrating ending, especially after the momentum Baton Rouge carried into Fresno following its sweep of Stockton.

Still, there's something to be said for finding a way to earn a point on the road against a quality opponent.

## A Road Trip of Ups and Downs

The Kingfish opened their California swing with consecutive victories over Stockton, improving to 5–2 and putting together their first three-game winning streak of the season.

Fresno brought that momentum to a halt.

Friday's loss featured a three-goal second period from the Falcons. Saturday was a tighter contest, but the result was another defeat.

Baton Rouge now sits at 5–3–1 with 11 standings points.

The Kingfish entered the weekend leading the Delta Division. The overtime point helps, although their exact position in the updated standings has not been independently confirmed.

The bigger issue may be consistency. Baton Rouge has demonstrated it can generate offense and compete with talented teams, but keeping opponents off the scoreboard remains a challenge.

And with another goaltending change Saturday night, the situation in the crease deserves continued attention.

## What's Next?

The Kingfish head east for three games against the Binghamton Black Bears, beginning Friday, November 13, at Visions Veterans Memorial Arena.

Baton Rouge will face Binghamton on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday, providing an immediate opportunity to get back into the win column.

Three games in three days will also test the team's depth and ability to recover quickly between contests.

The Kingfish leave California with two victories, two regulation losses, and one overtime loss.

That's not a disastrous road trip, but after the way things started in Stockton, it's hard not to feel that another point or two was there for the taking.

The good news? There's still plenty of hockey ahead, and Baton Rouge has already shown it can bounce back from a difficult weekend.

Let's see if they can do it again in Binghamton.
