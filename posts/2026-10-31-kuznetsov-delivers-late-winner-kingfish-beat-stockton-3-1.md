The Baton Rouge Kingfish waited until the final three minutes to take control Friday night, but they picked a pretty good time to do it.

Dmitry Kuznetsov scored with 2:45 remaining in regulation, breaking a 1–1 tie and lifting the Kingfish to a 3–1 victory over the Stockton Thunder at Adventist Health Arena.

The victory improves Baton Rouge to 4–2 and gives the Fish consecutive wins after dropping two straight against Mid-South last weekend. It also handed Stockton its first home loss of the season.

## A Late Breakthrough

Neither team found the net in the opening period, and Stockton struck first in the second.

Petr Panacek collected a loose puck in the right circle and fired it past Kingfish goaltender Ed Coffey for his fifth goal of the season.

Baton Rouge answered midway through the period when Kodiak Whiteduck finished a two-on-one opportunity set up by Sam Turner, tying the game at one.

From there, it became a goaltending battle.

Coffey and Stockton's William Augustine traded big saves as the game remained tied deep into the third period.

Then Kuznetsov changed everything.

His wrist shot from just inside the Stockton blue line slipped past Augustine, giving Baton Rouge its first lead of the evening with just 2:45 remaining.

Stockton pulled Augustine for an extra attacker, but Coffey held his ground, and Jake Cox finished things off with an empty-net goal with 25 seconds remaining.

Three goals, two points in the standings, and a pretty satisfying way to open a road trip.

## Coffey Comes Through Again

Ed Coffey was back between the pipes for Baton Rouge, and Friday's result was another encouraging performance from the Kingfish netminder.

The official league recap credits Coffey with several important stops, including during Stockton's late push with the extra attacker.

Augustine stopped 31 of 33 shots for Stockton. Coffey's exact save total was not included in the league report.

With Anthony Seykora having left last Saturday's game against Mid-South because of an injury, Coffey's continued presence in goal is particularly important. There has been no confirmed update on Seykora's condition.

## A Different Kind of Victory

The Kingfish opened their season with a pair of six-goal performances against Monroe, then put five on the board in Sunday's win over Mid-South.

Friday was different.

Baton Rouge had to stay patient, overcome an early deficit, and find a way to win a tight game late.

And that's an encouraging development.

Not every night is going to be a scoring festival. Sometimes the difference is a well-timed shot from the blue line, a goaltender making one more stop, and somebody putting the puck into an empty net.

This time, the Kingfish found all three.

At 4–2, Baton Rouge continues to strengthen its early position in the FPHL's Delta Division. The victory adds two standings points, though the precise updated division ranking still needs confirmation.

## What's Next?

The Kingfish and Thunder meet again Saturday, October 31, at Adventist Health Arena in Stockton. Puck drop is scheduled for 8:11 p.m. Central.

Baton Rouge then continues its California road trip with two games against the Fresno Falcons on November 6 and 7.

The Fish have now won two straight, including their first game of a seven-game road trip.

Friday night wasn't flashy, but it was the kind of win good teams need to find.

And Kuznetsov made sure they found it.
