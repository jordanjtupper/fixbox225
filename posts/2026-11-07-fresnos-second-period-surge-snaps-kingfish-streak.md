The Baton Rouge Kingfish saw their three-game winning streak come to an end Friday night at Selland Arena, falling 4–2 to the Fresno Falcons in the opening game of their two-game California series.

Baton Rouge entered the matchup riding high after sweeping Stockton last weekend. But Fresno, which came into Friday leading the Western Conference, showed why it has been one of the FPHL's early offensive powers.

The Kingfish struck first, taking a 1–0 lead into the first intermission. Fresno took control in the middle period, scoring three unanswered goals to turn that deficit into a 3–1 advantage.

Baton Rouge added another goal in the third, but Fresno answered with one of its own to preserve the two-goal margin.

The loss drops the Kingfish to 5–3–0, ending a three-game winning streak and leaving them with a 2–1 record through the first three games of their seven-game road trip.

For Baton Rouge, the bigger question is how quickly the team can respond. Fresno entered the series as the Western Conference's highest-scoring club, making Saturday's rematch another significant test for the Kingfish defense.

The loss leaves Baton Rouge at 5–3 with 10 standings points. The Kingfish entered Friday leading the Delta Division, and they'll look to protect that position in Saturday's rematch. Their updated divisional ranking after Friday's games has not yet been verified.

## Back at It Saturday

The two teams meet again Saturday, November 7, at Selland Arena, with puck drop scheduled for 8:05 p.m. Central. Baton Rouge will look to earn a weekend split before heading east for three games against Binghamton next weekend.

## Two Stories Worth Following

**1. Can the Kingfish contain Fresno's offense?**

Friday's three-goal second period offers an obvious area for improvement. Baton Rouge will need a more consistent defensive effort to leave California with a split.

**2. How will Baton Rouge respond to its first road loss?**

After consecutive victories in Stockton, the Kingfish now face a different challenge: recovering quickly against one of the conference's strongest early-season opponents.
