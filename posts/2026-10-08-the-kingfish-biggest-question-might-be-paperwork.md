We picked the Kingfish to win 30 games. Apparently, we should have asked who was going to be allowed to show up first.

Visa uncertainty around the FPHL has added a wrinkle to the season. Reports have raised questions about the league’s previous use of B-1 visitor status while efforts to secure P-1 athlete approvals continue. For fans, the question is simpler: Who can actually take the ice?

As of October 8, we haven’t found a public Kingfish announcement naming players held up by the process or giving a firm timetable. Being Canadian or European doesn’t automatically mean a player is unavailable. Until the team confirms individual situations, calling anyone out would be guessing.

Still, the potential impact is hard to ignore.

Start in goal. Noah Giesbrecht was a big part of the reason to expect improvement. If his availability is delayed and other goalie options are affected, Ed Coffey could become considerably more important. That’s a different opening assignment than competing for a spot in camp.

The same concern runs through the lineup. Ryan Olsen, Dawson McKinney and Narek Aleksanyan figure into the scoring picture. Kyle Soper and several other international players bring important defensive depth. We aren’t saying those guys will miss games. We’re saying their availability matters a whole lot more than whether the new jerseys look good.

It also puts more attention on Jake Cox, Tyler Larwood, Curtis Hansen and Scott Shorrock. If the roster is thin, familiar forwards will have to carry more of the offense, and younger players could get important minutes sooner than expected. We were already wondering about depth. This gives us another reason.

The hopeful outcome is a short disruption followed by players joining the lineup as approvals come through. The public information doesn’t support promising a date, though, and there’s no reason to assume everyone gets cleared together.

For now, the 30-win prediction stays. It just comes with a bigger condition. A couple of uneven weekends would be manageable. A month without important pieces would deserve a fresh look.

The Kingfish gave us reasons to be excited about this roster. Now we need to see how much of it is available when the puck drops.
